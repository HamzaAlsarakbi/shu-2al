# الندم (Al-Nadam, "The Regret"), Syrian drama, 2016

Writer: Hassan Sami Youssef (from his novel «عتبة الألم»). Director: Al-Laith Hajjo.
Damascus. The story runs from 2003 (the night Saddam Hussein's statue fell in Baghdad) to 2016;
pre-war scenes are in color, wartime scenes in black and white.

Orwa, a TV writer in his forties, narrates. His family, the Ghouls (آل الغول), are meat traders.
The father built the business from nothing; his sons compete over it. Abdo, the eldest, wants power
and a seat in parliament. Suhail, the middle son, left the business after clashing with the father and
resents Orwa, whom he sees as the favourite. Orwa turned his back on the money to become a writer.
